WHO WE ARE

About CampusCare Nigeria

CampusCare Nigeria is a student-focused platform created to address some of the everyday challenges faced by Nigerian students.

Our goal is to make educational information, scholarship opportunities, affordable food comparisons and student marketplace services easier to access.

Our Mission

To support Nigerian students with useful information, accessible opportunities and resources that make campus life easier.
